Evening Dining

Dinner is Served

Chilled beer · Local sake · Fine wine — let the evening begin.

  • Seasonally Changing Kaiseki

    Our menu is renewed every month to reflect the finest seasonal ingredients. Each visit offers a wholly new taste of the region.

  • Prepared to Order

    Every course is crafted fresh as you dine. Please allow approximately two hours to savour the full experience. For a quicker pace, let your attendant know.

  • Dietary Preferences

    If there are any ingredients you are unable to eat, please inform a member of staff before dinner begins and we will prepare an alternative with care.
